Know what you’re paying for.

Our payment guidance explains the checkout channel, consultation fee, custom project payments and how to get billing support.

Effective date: 28 November 2026 · Last updated: 28 November 2026

Please review the amount, service description and merchant details shown at checkout before authorising a transaction. Do not make a payment unless it relates to a service or consultation you intend to purchase.

1. Accepted payment channel

Online payments on this website are facilitated through Razorpay checkout. Available payment instruments are displayed by the payment provider at the time of checkout and may vary by customer, bank and transaction.

2. Consultation fee

The website may offer an initial IT consultancy and project onboarding session for ₹499. Confirm the displayed amount and description before proceeding. Payment confirms the transaction only; scheduling and consultation details may require separate communication.

3. Custom project payments

Custom amounts should be paid only after the service scope, amount, milestones and relevant invoice or written instructions have been agreed with Encode Technologies. A payment made without an agreed scope may require additional verification before it can be allocated.

4. Currency and transaction record

Website checkout is configured for Indian Rupees (INR). Keep the payment confirmation and transaction reference for your records. We may use payment references and status information to reconcile transactions and provide support.

5. Payment security

Payment details are handled by the payment provider through its checkout environment. Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or banking password with anyone claiming to represent us. Encode Technologies will not ask you to disclose these credentials.

6. Failed or pending transactions

If a transaction is pending, declined or debited without confirmation, avoid repeatedly submitting the payment until you have checked the status with your bank or payment provider. Contact us with the transaction reference so we can investigate.

7. Refunds and cancellations

Refund requests are reviewed under our Refund & Cancellation Policy, the applicable project agreement and mandatory law.

8. Billing support

For payment confirmation, invoice or billing questions, email encodetechnolgies@gmail.com or call +91 63673 58380. Include the transaction reference and date, but never send payment credentials.
