How engagements work

Each project is scoped individually. We begin by understanding your objectives and constraints, then propose deliverables, milestones, assumptions and an estimated schedule. Work begins after the scope and commercial terms are agreed.

Technology choices

Depending on the project, our engineering toolkit may include modern web frameworks, Android and cross-platform technologies, Firebase and cloud services, relational or NoSQL databases, REST/JSON APIs and third-party integrations. Specific tools are selected to suit the product.

What to include in your enquiry

  • The problem you want to solve and intended users.
  • Key features and any existing website, app or system.
  • Preferred timeline, budget range and launch priorities.
  • Any technical, compliance or integration requirements.
