Legal · Refunds

A transparent approach to payment requests.

This policy explains how to contact us about cancellations, refunds, failed transactions and billing concerns.

Effective date: 28 November 2026 · Last updated: 28 November 2026

Refund eligibility depends on the service purchased, work already performed, agreed milestones and applicable consumer law. This policy should be read with the invoice, proposal or written project agreement for the relevant transaction.

1. Scope

This policy applies to consultation fees and service payments collected by Encode Technologies through approved payment channels, including Razorpay checkout.

2. Consultation bookings

If a consultation needs to be rescheduled, contact us as early as possible using the official email or phone number. Where a consultation has not yet been provided, requests for cancellation or refund will be reviewed based on the booking details and applicable law.

3. Project and milestone payments

Project payments may cover discovery, planning, design, development, third-party costs or completed milestones. Amounts attributable to work already completed, approved deliverables or non-recoverable third-party costs may not be refundable, subject to applicable law and the written agreement. Any unearned balance will be assessed fairly.

4. How to request a refund

Email encodetechnolgies@gmail.com with your name, payment date, amount, transaction/reference ID, service description and reason for the request. Do not email card numbers, CVV or banking passwords.

5. Review and outcome

We will acknowledge a complete request and review the payment record, service status and agreed terms. We may request reasonable supporting details to verify the transaction. Approval or rejection will be communicated with the reason, subject to legal requirements.

6. Processing time

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be initiated to the original payment method through the payment provider. The time taken for the amount to appear depends on the provider and banking network. We will share available transaction or refund references.

7. Failed, duplicate or unauthorised payments

If money is debited but checkout does not show a successful payment, or if you believe a duplicate or unauthorised charge occurred, contact us promptly with the transaction reference. We will review the available payment records and coordinate with the payment provider where appropriate.

8. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights or remedies that cannot be excluded under applicable Indian law. Where this policy conflicts with mandatory law, the law will prevail.
